| **Météo-France AROME-PI** (`meteofrance_shortterm`), the "prévision immédiate" model, `aromepi/1.0` WCS on `public-api.meteofrance.fr`: hail diagnostic, gusts, visibility and type of precipitation, 24 steps of 15 minutes (6 hours), a run every hour, read as a map of France by the [vigie-meteo](https://github.com/Axellum/vigie-meteo) gateway | **Licence not verified.** Offered free of charge on the Météo-France portal; the one Open Data page of the model read (its precipitation types) names no licence, and the portal's own description page of the API was not read. | [Open Data page](https://confluence-meteofrance.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OpenDataMeteoFrance/pages/1674051588), [portal](https://portail-api.meteofrance.fr) | `Source: Météo-France, modèle AROME-PI (https://portail-api.meteofrance.fr), read by a vigie-meteo gateway; the levels (strong gusts, reduced visibility) and the kinds of precipitation are worked out by Vigie, not an official warning` (wording: ours) | 50 requests a minute on the portal for the key; the gateway makes 96 an hour (one per product and step, spaced out). This API asks the gateway for a point, cached 5 minutes per cell (a ceiling of our own, 3,000 points every 5 minutes). | Optional: on when `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_URL` is set. In the France box `GET /v1/point` carries `short_term` (the steps, the levels of gusts, hail and visibility, the types of precipitation). A model forecast: never part of `local_alert_level`, never an official warning (ADR 0031). |
