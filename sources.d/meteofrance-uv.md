The maximum UV index of today and tomorrow (real sky and clear sky) on a 0.025-degree grid; `ZoneWeather` and `/v1/point` show it as `uv_max` for those two days. The index of the current hour is **calculated by Vigie** from the maximum of the day and the height of the Sun (`sin(height) ** 1.2`, an approximation compared with Open-Meteo's index, not with a UV sensor). Later days keep Open-Meteo's `uv_max`. |
