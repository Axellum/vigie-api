Licence Ouverte 2.0` (wording: ours), then `Last update: <update_time>` | 50 requests a minute on the free plan of the Météo-France API portal (held to 90 % in `app/collectors/budget.py`); this API sends at most two requests (the map and the texts) every 3 minutes (cache 180 s). | The API key in `VIGIE_METEOFRANCE_API_KEY` goes in the `apikey` header, never in a URL, and is redacted in every error and log line. Each metropolitan department (`01` to `95`, `2A`, `2B`) and coastal strip (`<dep>10`, used for phenomenon `9` `Vagues-submersion`) is mapped to its `NUTS3` geocode from `app/data/geocodes-nuts.geojson.gz`, so a GPS point in France is matched offline and a department-wide `Crues` warning (phenomenon `4`) is deduplicated when a Vigicrues river section crosses the department at the same or a higher level. The follow-up bulletins (`textesvigilance/encours`) are returned in `texts` untouched. **Overseas territories (ADR 0036):** with the same key, the other entry points of `DPVigilance/v1` (`vigilanceom/flux/dernier`, a ZIP of about 1 MB with the maps of the Antilles, Guyane, La Réunion, Mayotte and the Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon bulletin; `polynesie/cartevigilance/encours`; `ncaledonie/cartevigilance/encours`) give the warnings of Guadeloupe, Martinique, Guyane, La Réunion, Mayotte, Saint-Martin, Saint-Barthélemy, Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon, French Polynesia and New Caledonia (`meteofrance_vigilance_overseas`, switched off with `VIGIE_METEOFRANCE_VIGILANCE_OVERSEAS=0`); at most three requests every 5 minutes; MeteoAlarm has no feed for these territories. Their area is the territory (code `MF_OM`, outline of `countries.geojson.gz` widened by 0.25 degree), because the sub-zones are published without a shape. The cyclone colours (5 and above) are listed without a Vigie level. **Not verified:** whether the API portal's general terms add conditions beyond Licence Ouverte 2.0 for the free key. |
