- **Offshore buoys of Météo-France** (`GET /v1/sea/buoys`, source `meteofrance_buoys`): the latest hourly record of the moored buoys of Météo-France (`DPObs/v2/bouees`; 10 buoys on 2026-11-16, in the Mediterranean, the Bay of Biscay and off the Antilles): significant wave height, mean period and direction, sea and air temperatures, wind and gust, sea-level pressure, with the date and the age of the record. Nearest first with `near_lat`, `near_lon` (and `radius_km`). Read at most every 30 minutes with `VIGIE_METEOFRANCE_API_KEY`; without it `status` is `not_configured`.
