- **The four 15-minute rain steps say which quarter-hours they are**: `rain_nowcast.mm_per_step` (`GET /v1/point`), the zones' `rain_next_60m`, the frame's `RAIN5M` and `ZONE` fields 16 to 18 and the Home Assistant sensor « rain in the next hour » were described as "the next hour". With Météo-France PIAF they are the hour from the current 5-minute boundary on. With Open-Meteo (outside France, and in France when PIAF gives no full hour) each value is the sum of the 15 minutes before its time: the first step is a quarter-hour already over, the one that ended when Open-Meteo answered, and an answer kept in cache can make it one quarter-hour older (two through the Météo-France gateway). The values themselves do not change.
