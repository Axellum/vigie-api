- `GET /v1/activities/{spot_id}` (ADR 0016): for a surf spot, a verdict hour by hour (`good`, `possible`, `poor`) for surf, kite or wing or windsurf, boat and shore fishing, with the criteria behind it, the best daylight slot of the next 24 hours, the tide (high and low waters, estimated coefficient) and the official coastal warnings that cap it (red: everything `poor`; orange: the boat and the shore fishing at `possible` at best). Every verdict is a rule of Vigie, its thresholds in `rules`; the kite's wind range is asked for (`wind_min_kn`, `wind_max_kn`). No request is added to a provider: it reads the surf forecast's answers. Not for safety.
