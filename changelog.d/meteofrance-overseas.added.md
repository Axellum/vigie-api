- **Weather of the overseas territories from Météo-France's AROME-OM** (optional, with `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_URL`; `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_OVERSEAS=0` turns it off): for a point in Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint-Martin, Saint-Barthélemy, French Guiana, La Réunion, Mayotte, New Caledonia, the Society, western Tuamotu or Austral islands, `GET /v1/point` takes the current conditions (temperature, humidity, wind, gusts, pressure, rain of the hour) and the complete local days from the model through the vigie-meteo gateway, credited to Météo-France; the rain probability, the UV maximum, the later days and the hourly cloud cover stay Open-Meteo's. The weather code is worked out by Vigie from the rain and the cloud cover (the model gives none) and the credit says so. Outside the windows, or when the gateway fails, Open-Meteo's public API gives the weather as before (ADR 0033).
