- **Tides**: `GET /v1/tides` corrects the modelled tide with the gap that the nearest REFMAR tide gauge (SHOM, Licence Ouverte 2.0, within 50 km, 11 gauges) measured in the last hour, fading over 12 hours (ADR 0017). The new `correction` field says whether it was applied, from which gauge and measure, or why not; `correct=false` gives the model alone. Measured on 14 days it lowers the error of the heights by 7 to 14 % (+1 to +6 h) and does not move the times of the high and low waters. The coefficient stays an estimate.
