- **The Map page of the Home Assistant card** (ADR 0048): every spot of the instance on a map, coloured by the verdict of
  the activity chosen (surf, kite, boat, shore fishing) as on the website, the best with the figure of their hour and the
  arrow of the tide, and, closer in, the bathing sites, ports, slipways, anchorages and dive sites of the French coast. A tap
  opens a sheet: the four verdicts and the hour of a spot, a star that keeps it among the beaches of the Sea page (6 at most,
  shared by every screen at once), and a button to its Sea page; the header of a beach on the Sea page has the same star, and
  the editor reads and writes the same list. The background is OpenFreeMap's, in Home Assistant's theme; without WebGL2 or
  the Internet the page says it cannot draw the map.
