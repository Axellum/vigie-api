- **Surf**: the forecast swell was measured against the CANDHIS wave buoys over 11 months (ADR 0022, `tools/measure_surf_error.py`, `docs/measurements/surf-error-2026-11.md`). The forecast follows the sea (correlation 0.93 to 0.96; root mean square error 0.38 m at 1 day against 0.92 m for persistence) and reads about 10 % low, and 12 % low over 3 m. One factor per spot lowers the held-out error by 38.5 % at 1 day and 25.2 % at 5 days, at 8 of the 19 spots that have a buoy within 30 km, but nothing is served on it: the API does not change, and building the correction is the owner's decision.
