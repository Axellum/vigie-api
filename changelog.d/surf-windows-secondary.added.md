- **Surf session windows, secondary swell power and angle, and optional tide and swell direction preferences (`GET /v1/surf/spots/{spot_id}`)**: each hour of a spot's forecast now includes the deep-water power and angle to the beach of the secondary swell (`secondary_swell_power_kw_m`, `secondary_swell_angle_deg`), the forecast lists the runs of consecutive daylight hours (`windows`) where the rating reaches at least half of what could be scored, and the caller can pass `best_tide` (`low`, `mid`, `high` or `any`) and `best_swell_from_deg` (0 to 359) to score the tide stage and the swell direction against them.
