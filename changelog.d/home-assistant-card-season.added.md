- **Seasonal lines on the dashboard card** (Home Assistant integration 0.7.0): avalanches, drought, pollen and forest fires are shown only when there is something to say. The strongest line is a pill of the home page (with "+n" for the others) that opens the Alerts page, where each line has its block: the danger of the avalanche bulletin on its five-step scale with its area, problems and end; the drought restrictions of VigiEau by kind of water (a watch without restriction is not shown); the pollen species reaching 1 grain/m³ over the next days, in a neutral colour since the CAMS model gives no health threshold; the forest fire danger of Météo-France from "moderate" (it is published from June to September, read by the integration once an hour from `/v1/forest-fire`).
