- **The rain steps give their start**: `rain_nowcast.start_utc` (`GET /v1/point`) and the zones' `rain_next_60m_start_utc` are the start of the first 15-minute step (UTC epoch seconds); with Météo-France PIAF it is also the start of the first 5-minute rate. A client no longer has to guess it from `server_utc`, which was one or two quarter-hours off when Open-Meteo's answer came from the cache. The Home Assistant sensor « rain in the next hour » gives it as the attribute `start_utc`.
