- **Probabilities of rain, snow, gusts, hail and storm from Météo-France's ensemble forecasts** (`probabilities` of `GET /v1/point`, source `meteofrance_ensemble`, optional, with `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_URL`): in metropolitan France and Corsica, PE-AROME gives, in windows of three hours up to 51 hours, the probability that the rain reaches 1 mm and 5 mm, that snow reaches 1 mm, and the probability of a gust over 30 and 50 knots, of hail and of a strong shower or thunderstorm; PE-ARPEGE gives, in windows of 24 hours up to 96 hours, the probability that the rain reaches 1, 5, 10 and 20 mm (counted among its 35 members by the gateway). `rain_peak_next_24h` is the most likely three hours of the next day. The compact profile keeps the next 24 hours of PE-AROME; `compact=false` gives all. A model forecast: never part of `local_alert_level`, never an official warning; `daily[].rain_probability_pct` stays Open-Meteo's and is not merged with these.
