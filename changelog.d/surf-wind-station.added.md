- **Coastal weather stations and 6-minute wind observations for surf spots** (`GET /v1/surf/stations`, `GET /v1/surf/spots/{spot_id}/station`, `app/collectors/meteofrance_obs.py`, `app/services/stations.py`, `app/data/meteofrance-coastal-stations.json`): bundles the 77 open real-time Météo-France coastal stations within 35 km of the surf spots (`POSTES_MF.csv`, Licence Ouverte 2.0) and reads the 6-minute observations package (`DPPaquetObs/v1/paquet/infrahoraire-6m`, enabled via `VIGIE_METEOFRANCE_OBS_API_KEY` or `VIGIE_METEOFRANCE_API_KEY`) to report real-time 10 m wind speed, gusts, direction, relative wind regime (`offshore`, `cross-shore`, `onshore`), air temperature, humidity, pressure and 6-minute precipitation at the nearest station, with optional live comparison (`compare=true`) against the spot's hourly wind forecast.
