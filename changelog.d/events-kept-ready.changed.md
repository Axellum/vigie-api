- **Events**: `GET /v1/events` keeps its answer, already serialized, and serves it again while nothing it was made from has changed: no feed read again or expired, no warning in it ended, no simulation or watched-zone change. A world view of 4.9 MB took 117 ms to work out and write at every call; a kept one takes 0.3 ms (measured on synthetic data of that size, in the application only). The answer is the same byte for byte; `server_utc` and `fetched_age_s` are those of the moment it was worked out. A failing source is still asked again at every call, unless its circuit holds it back (an open circuit, or a WMO registry service waiting after its failures): then the answer holds until the source would be asked. `GET /v1/health` gains `events_cache` (kept, hits, misses, not kept, and why an answer was not kept or was dropped: the reason and the provider).
