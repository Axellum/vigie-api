- **The last days a station measured** (`GET /v1/climate/recent`, optional, with `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_URL`): minimum, maximum and mean temperature, rain of the day, mean wind and highest gust of the last days (up to 14) at the nearest Météo-France station that still measures (the stations of `GET /v1/climate/normals`, then the next two when it has none), from the daily climatological data of Météo-France through the vigie-meteo gateway, with the mean of the month to set them against. A measure Météo-France marks doubtful is left out and named. France and overseas. ADR 0034.
