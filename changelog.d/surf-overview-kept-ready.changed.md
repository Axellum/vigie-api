- **Surf overview**: the scheduler keeps `GET /v1/surf/overview` ready. It reads the two every-spot answers again a few minutes before they expire and works the overview out as soon as the hour turns, so that no visitor waits for it (the first visitor after an expiry or after the turn of the hour waited about 1.5 s of computing on a PC, 3.3 s measured on the server). Same cache entries and same requests as the route (at most 9 + 9 a day); nothing is read while nobody asked for an overview within the last day, and without `VIGIE_SCHEDULER=1` nothing changes.
