- **The dashboard card appears with the others, also after a restart** (Home Assistant integration 0.8.0, ADR 0044): where the dashboards keep their resources in storage and the configuration has a `www` folder, the integration copies the card to `www/vigie/` (with a compressed copy) and lists it itself as a dashboard resource, like the cards of HACS, so a dashboard opened while Home Assistant starts no longer shows "Configuration error" until it is reloaded; elsewhere it is loaded as before. The card also keeps its last answer in the browser and draws it at once while it asks again (never an answer older than 30 minutes, and a request that fails shows its error); measured on 2026-11-24 it came 37 ms after the other cards on the local network and 230 to 350 ms after them on a mobile network. Removing the last Vigie entry removes the resource and the copy.
