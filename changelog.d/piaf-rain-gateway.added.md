- **Rain over the next hour from Météo-France PIAF in France** (optional): with `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_URL` pointing at a [vigie-meteo](https://github.com/Axellum/vigie-meteo) gateway, a point in France gets PIAF's 5-minute rain instead of Open-Meteo's 15-minute steps: `rain_nowcast.mm_per_step` and `rain_next_60m` hold their sums by quarter of an hour, the new `rain_nowcast.rate_5m_mm_h` and `ZoneWeather.rain_rate_5m_mm_h` the twelve 5-minute rates, and the frame's `RAIN5M` line carries them (same format). When the gateway does not give the twelve steps ahead, Open-Meteo's rain stays. The source is credited (`meteofrance_piaf`) and listed in `disabled_sources` when off.
