- **Météo-France avalanche-risk bulletins (BRA) for the French massifs** (`GET /v1/avalanches` and `PointResponse.avalanche`): a third source, `meteofrance_bra`, after SLF and Euregio. The 35 massifs of the Alps, Corsica and the Pyrenees are matched offline (`app/data/meteofrance-bra-massifs.geojson.gz`), the bulletin of a massif is read once every 30 minutes (Licence Ouverte 2.0, `DPBRA/v1`) and returned in French with the danger by elevation band, the exposed aspects, the typical avalanche situations (derived: codes 3 and 5 not confirmed), the activity and snowpack texts and the next-day outlook. It needs `VIGIE_METEOFRANCE_API_KEY`: without it the source is listed in `disabled_sources` and a point in a massif answers `status: unavailable`. `?source=meteofrance` forces it.
