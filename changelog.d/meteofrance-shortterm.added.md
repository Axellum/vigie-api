- **Hail, gusts, reduced visibility and type of precipitation of the next hours** (`short_term` of `GET /v1/point`, source `meteofrance_shortterm`, optional, with `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_URL`): in metropolitan France and Corsica, Météo-France's AROME-PI model in steps of 15 minutes up to 6 hours: the strongest gust, the hail diagnostic, the lowest visibility and the type of precipitation (rain, drizzle, snow, wet snow, rain and snow, freezing, ice pellets, snow pellets, hail) of each step, with a level worked out for gusts (60 and 90 km/h, ours), hail (8 and 16 kg/m2, Météo-France's own thresholds) and visibility (under 1,000 and 200 m, ours) and the first moment each is reached. A model forecast, **not an official warning**: never part of `local_alert_level`, credited to Météo-France, licence not verified (ADR 0031). The compact profile (the default) carries the levels and the credit, with an empty `steps`; `compact=false` adds the 24 steps.
