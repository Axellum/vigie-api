- **Live comparison of a CANDHIS wave buoy against the MFWAM forecast (`GET /v1/surf/spots/{spot_id}/buoy?compare=true`)**: when `compare=true` and the nearest buoy has a current record (`status` is `ok`), `comparison` compares the buoy's measurement with the spot's MFWAM wave forecast at the nearest hour within 30 minutes (`model_wave_height_m`, `height_diff_m`, `height_ratio`, `model_wave_period_s`, `model_swell_height_m`, `model_swell_period_s`, `model_swell_from_deg`, `direction_diff_deg`).
