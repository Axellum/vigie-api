- **Radar of Météo-France over France** (`GET /v1/radar/france`, `GET /v1/radar/france/tiles/{epoch}/{z}/{x}/{y}.png`, source `meteofrance_radar`, optional, with `VIGIE_METEO_GATEWAY_URL`): the rain the radars of Météo-France measured over metropolitan France (`DPRadar`, mosaic `LAME_D_EAU`, 500 m), one image every 5 minutes, the last three hours kept, as 256 px Web Mercator tiles drawn by the vigie-meteo gateway and relayed here (immutable, cached for a day), with the dates of the images, the area, and the intensity classes and their colours. For a point in France, `rain_nowcast.radar_tile_url_template` now points to the newest image (a path relative to this API, instead of RainViewer's URL, which stays outside France when it is on) and the credit is Météo-France's.
