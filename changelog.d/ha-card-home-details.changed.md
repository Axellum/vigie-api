- **The home page and the Details page of the dashboard card, redrawn** (Home Assistant integration): the card has a fixed size, 760 px high (12 rows of the sections view), and a page longer than that scrolls inside it. The home page gives now with the strongest gust to come, the pressure and its trend, the air quality, the UV and the sun times, the rain of the next hour in 12 bars of 5 minutes (a tile that opens Details), the next 24 hours by 2 hours and the next 7 days (10 on a wall screen) as columns tinted by their sun, grey to gold and navy at night, with the current hour and today ringed; it no longer has a line of credits, which the other pages keep. The Details page gives the rain until tomorrow noon in a few periods, the next hour by 5 minutes, the next hours by quarter hour, 48 hours hour by hour and the chances per 3 hours, with rows for snow, strong gusts and hail only when a window has some. The warning pill of the header names its phenomenon ("Orange wind").
