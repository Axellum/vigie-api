- **The rain of the next hour is read at your own kilometre** (Home Assistant integration 0.6.0): the Météo-France PIAF rain is asked of the gateway on its own grid of 0.01 degree (about 1 km, `VIGIE_GRID_RAIN_NOWCAST_DEG`) instead of the 5 km forecast grid, so a shower smaller than the forecast cell is not missed. The Home Assistant sensor « rain in the next hour » also gives the twelve 5-minute rates (`rate_5m_mm_h`) and the nine slots of the former Météo-France API (`slots`: 5 minutes for the first half hour, then 10), in metropolitan France.
