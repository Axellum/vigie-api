- **Home Assistant: a dashboard card ships with the integration** (ADR 0042): the weather, the warnings, the sea and the normals of the place on five pages (Home, Details, Alerts, Sea, Normal?) one swipes or picks in a bar, in French and English, dark and light, phone and wall layouts. Home Assistant loads it by itself (no resource to add); a page with nothing for the place is not shown. The integration also carries its brand images, and `tools/fake_instance.py --replay` serves recorded weather answers moved to now, which the new `card` job of the Home Assistant workflow uses to try the card in a real Home Assistant.
