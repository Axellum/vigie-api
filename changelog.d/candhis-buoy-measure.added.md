- **Surf**: `GET /v1/surf/spots/{id}/buoy` gives the latest record (significant height, period, direction at the peak, sea temperature) of the nearest CANDHIS wave buoy (Cerema, Licence Ouverte 2.0) that has a current one, with the date of the record (UTC), its age and the buoy's distance; `comparable` is true within 30 km. It needs a key (`VIGIE_CANDHIS_API_KEY`, asked of the Candhis team, sent in a header and never shown): without one the route says `not_configured` and `GET /v1/health` lists the source in `disabled_sources`. CANDHIS is read with three requests an hour at most (150 a day allowed), whatever the number of clients.
