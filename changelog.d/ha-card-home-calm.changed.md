- **A calmer home page for the dashboard card** (Home Assistant integration): colour only where it says something. The
  temperatures keep the colour of their value, the rain its blue, a gust of 60 km/h or more its yellow or orange; the wind
  of "now", of the hours and of the days is no longer blue but in the colour of the text or grey. The wind of each day
  says its unit (km/h) and its gusts go on a line of their own ("gusts 25") instead of "13/25"; the rain of a day is a
  gauge, the same width every day, filled by its amount, above that amount. Where the day columns are too narrow
  for "↖ 28 km/h" (a phone), the wind of the day keeps its unit and leaves out its arrow (its direction is in the hours
  and on Details).
