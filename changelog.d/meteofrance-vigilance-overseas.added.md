- **Official Météo-France vigilance of the overseas territories** (`GET /v1/vigilances?country=GP|MQ|GF|RE|YT|MF|BL|PM|PF|NC` and position queries there): the same routes, Vigie levels and archive as for France, read from the other entry points of `DPVigilance/v1` with the same key (`vigilanceom/flux/dernier`, `polynesie/…`, `ncaledonie/…`; at most three requests every 5 minutes). A warning covers its territory (the sub-zones have no published shape). The cyclone colours are listed without a Vigie level. Switch off with `VIGIE_METEOFRANCE_VIGILANCE_OVERSEAS=0` (ADR 0036).
